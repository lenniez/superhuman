  • Project planning: creating up-front, end-to-end project plans with owners of each step is complex, detailed, and time-consuming.
  • Daily morning triage: important requests are scattered across email, texts, DMs and voicemails, so urgent items get buried and it takes time to figure out what needs attention.
  • Continuous coordination on the go: commitments made mid-call or between appointments aren't captured well or are disorganized, because there's little time to write them down. Promised follow-ups may get forgotten.
  • Getting paid: invoicing, payment chasing and paperwork get pushed to evenings or delayed, slowing cash flow and eating into personal time, causing burnout.
  • Prospecting (ongoing): lead follow-ups and business development fall through the cracks when client work spikes, creating feast-or-famine income cycles.

05 · Potential solutions

Three solutions explored

Solution 1
Cross-channel task collector & list generator
Agent parses conversations and generates a task list in real time
EmailPhoneVideoLiveTextsSlackTeams
Solution 2
One-press voice / text capture
Action Button or shortcut → speak → captured, no unlocking
  • Enriched with calendar and location
  • Prompts for due date, collaborators
Solution 3
Agent-owned task category
Agent listens for preset, approved tasks and handles them
  • Simple email / message responses
  • Scheduling meetings
  • 1. Cross-channel task collector & list generator: agent integrates with cross-channel discussions and generates / updates the task list in real time. Parses email, phone, video or live conversation transcripts, texts, or Slack/Teams to gather tasks, deadlines, and roughly prioritize. Generates a suggested board (Backlog, To Do Today, In Progress, Blocked, Done) or calendar view the user can interact with.
  • 2. One-press voice/text capture: map the iPhone Action Button, Android shortcut, or laptop shortcut to instant voice capture. One press, speak the tasks, captured, without unlocking or opening apps. Enriched with recent calendar invites or location; user may be prompted for due date, collaborators, requesters; tasks can be sent to Trello, Notion, documents.
  • 3. Agent-owned task category: agent listens to specific channels for preset tasks it can handle independently and that the user has approved, minimizing tasks the user needs to track: simple email/message responses, scheduling meetings.
